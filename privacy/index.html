Privacy & disclosures

Updated September 29, 2026

Getting in touch

The email links on this website open your email app. A message is sent only when you send it from your email app. Hatz uses the information you choose to send to respond to your question, recommendation, or collaboration inquiry. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

Videos and social platforms

The latest-video section is updated automatically from Hatz’s public YouTube channel, and its thumbnails load from YouTube. Place pages include YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embedded player. When one of those pages loads, your browser connects to YouTube. Playing a video or following a social link may involve data collection by that provider under its own policies. You can read Google’s privacy policy or use the direct YouTube link on each page.

The map

The map page loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and a map library from unpkg. When it loads, your browser connects to those services, which may see your IP address. The map does not ask for or use your location. The Directions buttons open Google Maps in a new tab.

Website operation

This website does not add its own advertising pixels, analytics cookies, newsletter signup, or stored contact forms. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site.

Reviews and collaborations

Hatz publishes local food, business, and experience content. Paid, gifted, affiliate, and relevant personal relationships should be disclosed with the content where they apply. A collaboration does not guarantee a positive opinion. The Galactic Crunch Co. feature identifies its family connection on the video page.

Questions or corrections

For privacy questions, content corrections, or collaboration inquiries, email Hatz@hatzmedia.com.
