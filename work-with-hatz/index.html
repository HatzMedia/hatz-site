WAYS TO WORK TOGETHER

Four ways in.

Three are filmed at your place. The fourth comes to Hatz. All of them are in his own voice, for the people who already follow along.

01

Food & restaurant features

A menu, a new opening, a signature bite, or the thing regulars already know about.

02

Local business stories

The people, products and ideas behind your business, for shops, makers, markets and services.

03

Events & experiences

A festival, an attraction, a seasonal event or an experience to bring the crew along for.

04

Product reviews

Something new from a local or Ohio maker, tried on camera at home. Mail it or Hatz picks it up. The pin still lands on your shop.

What every collaboration includes

  • An on-site visit, or your product in hand, and a video made for the phone, posted to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
  • A permanent place on the Hatz map: your own page with the video, directions and a pin people find later.
  • The clean video file to use on your own channels, with the usage terms written down.
  • A clear paid-partnership label on every post, so viewers keep trusting the reviews and you.

HOW IT WORKS

From email to posted video.

Simple on purpose. Most of it happens in one call.

  1. EmailSend your business name, location and what you’d like people to know. A date in mind helps.
  2. One quick callScope, timing and any paid partnership details are agreed in writing before work begins.
  3. The visitHatz films on site. You’ll know the date; the visit itself stays natural.
  4. PostedVideos go up within 2 to 4 weeks of the last day of filming. Need it sooner? A rush option is available; ask when you email.

Keeping it honest

People trust Hatz because he says what he thinks. Paid work is always labeled, the opinions stay his own, and a collaboration does not buy a verdict. If a visit doesn’t go the way either of us hoped, we talk before anything is posted.

WHERE HATZ GOES

Cincinnati and the whole state.

Most visits are in Clermont and Hamilton counties and the Northern Kentucky river towns, and road trips are welcome: the crew has filmed as far as Sandusky and Lima. Travel is simply part of the quote. Product reviews can come from anywhere in Ohio: mail it, or Hatz picks it up.

WHAT IT COSTS

Starting points are shared by email.

Pricing depends on what you want to make, where it is, and how you’ll use the video afterwards. Every collaboration starts with a conversation, and nothing is booked until the scope and the number are agreed in writing.

FOR CREATORS

Make something together.

Two creators at one place beats one. If you make food, family or Ohio content, Hatz is up for it.

Joint visits

Pick a spot, film it together, both post. The place lands on the Hatz map, and your crowd meets his.

Festival games

The action-figure fights and ring-toss challenges are built for a second player. Bring your crew and lose gracefully.

Road trips

Cedar Point, Kelleys Island, a diner in Lima. Split the drive, double the footage.

You pick, Hatz picks

Each of you names a place the other has never been to. Film both reactions, post both.

The ground rules

  • Family-friendly, always. Kids watch this.
  • Honest on both sides. Nobody’s paid to like a place, and if a business is paying either of us, it’s labeled.
  • Both post, both tag. Hatz posts to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
  • Anywhere around Cincinnati works, and Ohio road trips are welcome. The map already has 17 of them.
