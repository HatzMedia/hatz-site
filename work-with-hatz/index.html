  • An on-site visit, or your product in hand, and the full Hatz review video posted to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, plus any shorts you add.

HOW IT WORKS

From email to posted video.

Simple on purpose. Most of it happens in one call.

  1. EmailSend your business name, location and what you’d like people to know. A date in mind helps.
  2. One quick callScope, timing and any paid partnership details are agreed in writing before work begins.
  3. The visitHatz films on site. You’ll know the date; the visit itself stays natural.
  4. PostedVideos go up within 2 to 4 weeks of the last day of filming. Need it sooner? A rush option is available; ask when you email.

Keeping it honest

People trust Hatz because he says what he thinks. Paid work is always labeled, the opinions stay his own, and a collaboration does not buy a verdict. If a visit doesn’t go the way either of us hoped, we talk before anything is posted.

WHERE HATZ GOES

About an hour from Cincinnati’s east side, and farther by arrangement.

Most visits are in Clermont and Hamilton counties and the Northern Kentucky river towns. The crew has filmed as far as Sandusky and Lima, so ask. Product reviews can come from anywhere in Ohio: mail it, or Hatz picks it up.

WHAT IT COSTS

Starting points are shared by email.

Pricing depends on what you want to make, where it is, and how you’ll use the video afterwards. Every collaboration starts with a conversation, and nothing is booked until the scope and the number are agreed in writing.

FOR CREATORS

Make something together.

Two creators at one place beats one. If you make food, family or Ohio content, Hatz is up for it.

Joint visits

Pick a spot, film it together, both post. The place lands on the Hatz map, and your crowd meets his.

Festival games

The action-figure fights and ring-toss challenges are built for a second player. Bring your crew and lose gracefully.

Guest spots

Jump into a Hatz video, or have Hatz in yours. A taste test, a cameo, a rematch.

You pick, Hatz picks

Each of you names a place the other has never been to. Film both reactions, post both.

The ground rules

  • Family-friendly, always. Kids watch this.
  • Honest on both sides. Nobody’s paid to like a place, and if a business is paying either of us, it’s labeled.
  • Both post, both tag. Hatz posts to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.
  • The Cincinnati area is easiest. Ohio road trips by arrangement.
